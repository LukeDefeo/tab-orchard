A local-first Chrome extension

Grow your tabs into a working tree.

Tab Orchard preserves the relationship between tabs, adds fast keyboard navigation, and works with Chrome’s native tab groups.

Designed around how tabs begin

Structure without surrendering Chrome.

Tab Orchard builds an organiser around the browser features you already use.

Parent-child tab trees

Links opened from a page stay attached to their source, and complete branches can be moved or closed together.

Native tab groups

Top-level branches can be linked to Chrome groups while preserving their internal tree structure.

Keyboard-first picker

Jump through recently used tabs or navigate the full tree without leaving the keyboard.

Local by design

Tab state stays inside the Chrome profile. There is no account, analytics service, advertising or remote server.

Your browsing stays on your device.

Tab Orchard uses browser permissions only to organise open tabs and provide features you explicitly choose.
