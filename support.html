Support

For questions, bug reports or feature requests, use Tab Orchard’s public GitHub issue tracker. Please do not include private URLs, page titles, screenshots or confidential workplace information in a report.

Open a support request

Useful details to include

  • Your Chrome or Chromium browser name and version.
  • Your operating system version.
  • A short sequence of actions that reproduces the problem.
  • Whether the issue occurs in the side panel, quick picker or settings.
